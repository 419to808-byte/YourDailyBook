Write Your Own — for writers

Turn your idea into a book you can sell

Write Your Own is a tool for writers. Bring your idea — the AI writes the book chapter by chapter, you revise it with a guided question set, and you leave with a finished book and a license to sell it on Amazon KDP, Gumroad, or anywhere else. You keep the revenue.

Who is this book for?

Child mode

One rule applies in every mode, always: no sexual content involving minors — ever.

  1. 1

    Describe your book

    Your idea, your characters, your world — plus a short description of yourself. The AI builds a story bible from it so the book stays consistent.

  2. 2

    The AI writes it, chapter by chapter

    One chapter at a time, following the story bible. You watch each chapter land and approve it before the next one starts.

  3. 3

    Revise with 15 guided questions

    After each chapter, answer questions about plot, dialogue, pacing, emotion, and more — in your own words, in open text boxes. The AI revises from your direction.

  4. 4

    Leave with a Rough Draft + a license to sell

    Your book is delivered as a rough draft — never as a final, publication-ready product. Final editing is your job (or your editor's). And you get the license to sell it and keep every dollar.

Rough Draft

A book made with Write Your Own is released as a rough draft, not a final product. You are responsible for final editing, proofreading, and preparation for publication. Our revision system gets the draft as close to final as possible — but the final polish is yours.

    What a $5 revision includes

    • Your feedback, applied — your question answers + open text direct the rewrite.
    • 3 different revised versions to choose from — e.g., dialogue-focused, description-focused, balanced. Pick the best, or mix parts.
    • A full quality check on the version you pick — continuity vs. the story bible, dialogue, pacing, emotion, description, grammar.
    • A change summary — what changed and why.
    • Free self-editing after picking — tweak the text yourself for free. You only pay again for another full AI revision round.

    1 revision per chapter is included with your book. Extra revisions are $5 per chapter — and there's no cap, as long as each one is paid for. Your book-in-progress is saved, so you can leave and pick up exactly where you left off, anytime. No time limit, no expiry.
